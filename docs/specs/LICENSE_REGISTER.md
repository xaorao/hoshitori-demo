# Licence register

Scope: what we may adapt, what we may only learn from, what we depend on. Status: the clone table is generated; the handling decisions are the project's standing rule (MIT, Apache and BSD may be adapted with a credit in `ryo-agent-league/NOTICE.md`; everything else is ideas only and rebuilt from a behaviour spec). **This is not legal advice; ambiguous cases are marked and resolved conservatively (ideas only).**

## 1. Generated facts

A register kept during the study lists every cloned repository (101 directories), its remote, the commit that was checked out and the licence identified from the licence file text by a script that was then corrected by hand (the script reported axe-core as GPL because the Mozilla Public License text names GPL as a secondary licence; it is MPL-2.0). Counts after correction: MIT 39, Apache-2.0 30, no licence file found 16, LGPL-3.0 3, BSD-2-Clause 2, MPL-2.0 2, BSD-3-Clause 1, GPL-3.0 1, AGPL-3.0 2, Elastic-2.0 1, Creative Commons 2 (OWASP ASVS and cheat sheets), other documentation licences 2 (W3C and the Community Specification License). The commit pin is the HEAD at clone time; licence text was read at that commit. A later change of licence is not tracked until a dependency is chosen, at which point the exact version's licence is re-read.

## 4. Open licence questions (not resolved by reading)

Raw RYO payload redistribution (organisers' answer pending); each exchange's terms for redistribution of market data; Telegram and Discord terms for automated reading; the Binance announcement feed terms; the licence of the Etherscan label data inside eth-labels; model-provider retention and training terms (provider not chosen). Each stays open until its owner answers or the source is dropped.

## 5. Process

Before any dependency enters the lockfile: record name, version, licence text read at that version, transitive licences from the dependency-review tool, and the reason; reject any strong-copyleft runtime dependency; keep the README disclosure list and `ryo-agent-league/NOTICE.md` generated from the lockfile and the register, and checked in CI against the lockfile so they cannot drift.
