# Findings against the forecast and settlement contract

The architecture work tests the contract (version <!--n:cver-->0.16<!--/n-->) against primary sources and experiments. A finding here is a place where the contract is wrong, silent or unverified, with the evidence and the proposed amendment. Amendments are applied to the contract in the synchronisation step (plan 17, step 7) as a new version, each citing its finding. Nothing here changes a product rule on its own authority.
